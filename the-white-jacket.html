Sharp Lines: The Levye Cropped White Jacket

A classic cropped jacket, rebuilt with a street edge.

The collar leads

A high stand collar lined with silver grommets gives the jacket its attitude. It is the one detail that says streetwear before anything else does.

Tailoring underneath

Panelled seams shape the shoulders and chest, and a pointed hem opens the front into a sharp V. A single hook closure keeps the line clean.

How to wear it

Pair it with dark trousers and a plain tee for contrast, or layer it over a shirt for a more formal look. Replace this with your own material, sizing and styling notes.
